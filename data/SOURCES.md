# KidneyCare source catalog

The project proposal identifies CDC and NIDDK as authoritative sources for the fixed healthcare document collection.

## Sources

1. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (2024). Chronic Kidney Disease Basics.
   https://www.cdc.gov/kidney-disease/about/index.html

2. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (2026, March 31). Chronic Kidney Disease in the United States.
   https://www.cdc.gov/kidney-disease/php/data-research/index.html

3. National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases. What Is Chronic Kidney Disease in Adults?
   https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/chronic-kidney-disease-ckd/what-is-chronic-kidney-disease.

The included demo_ckd.txt is a compact demonstration knowledge base based on topics covered by these sources. The RAG application does not fetch these websites at runtime.
